How the price works

You hear the number before the truck moves.

Junk removal is priced by volume, which is a fancy way of saying how much of the trailer your stuff takes up. One cubic yard is about the size of a washing machine. A quarter load is a few pieces of furniture. A full load is most of a garage.

Most companies will not tell you anything until they are standing in your driveway, and plenty of people find the price has climbed by then. We would rather send you a number first. Text a photo, get the price, decide with no one standing in front of you.

Questions people ask first

Straight answers.

How much does it cost?
It depends on how much of the truck your load fills, which is why we ask for a photo. Send one by text and you will get a firm number back before we drive out. No charge for asking, and no obligation.
Can you come today?
Often yes, if we still have room on the truck. Call or text and we will tell you straight away rather than booking you in and hoping.
Do I have to move anything to the curb?
No. It can stay where it is, upstairs, in the attic, or behind the shed. Carrying it out is the job.
What will you not take?
Anything hazardous: paint, chemicals, asbestos, and similar. Ask us about a specific item and we will tell you honestly instead of turning up and refusing it.
Which areas do you cover?
Frisco, Plano, McKinney, Prosper, Allen and Little Elm, plus the surrounding North Dallas suburbs. If you are close to the edge of that, ask.
Do you clean up afterwards?
Yes. We sweep the space before we leave. You should not be able to tell where the pile was.
