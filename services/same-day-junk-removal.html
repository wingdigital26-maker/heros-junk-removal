Frequently asked questions

Is there a rush fee for same-day pickup?

No. Same-day is quoted exactly like every other job, approved before we load. The one thing speed cannot change is household hazardous waste, which goes to a city collection program instead of on the trailer. The TCEQ guide for Texas households explains why.

What is the cutoff for same-day?

Call before noon and same-day usually works across most of DFW. After noon, worst case is typically the next morning's first slot.

Do you do same-day on weekends?

Yes, 7 days a week, 7am to 7pm. Weekend slots go fastest, so call early.

What areas qualify?

The northern DFW suburbs: Plano, Allen, Frisco, McKinney, Richardson, Little Elm, Prosper, and the cities between.
