Mattress Removal, Frisco

A mattress is one of the most common single-item calls we get from Frisco, almost always tied to a move rather than the mattress simply wearing out. We carry it down from wherever it sits, including a third-floor apartment, and route it to donation when it still qualifies.

What a mattress removal pickup actually involves

Mattress removal in a transient, fast-growing city like Frisco is mostly a timing problem, not a condition problem. A family moving into a new build often has a mattress from the old house that does not match the new bedroom's setup, or a delivery is scheduled and the old mattress has to be gone before the new one arrives. Texas does not run a statewide mattress recycling program, so a mattress that cannot be donated gets separated for its recyclable steel coils and foam where a processor accepts it, rather than defaulting straight to the landfill. A mattress and box spring together are still handled as one pickup, carried flat through stairwells and hallways with the frame protected the whole way.

What makes Frisco different

Frisco's master-planned neighborhoods, like Phillips Creek Ranch and Newman Village, generally have wide hallways and standard door clearances, so getting a mattress out of a bedroom is rarely the obstacle. What is worth mentioning up front is a scheduled delivery: tell us the delivery window for the new mattress and we will time the old one's pickup so the room is clear before the new one shows up, which matters more in a city where move-in and move-out days are constant.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a real mattress recycling program in Frisco or Texas?

No statewide mattress recycling program exists in Texas. A mattress that cannot be donated is separated for recyclable steel and foam where a processor accepts it, rather than going straight to a landfill by default.

Can you time a Frisco pickup around a new mattress delivery?

Yes. Tell us the delivery window and we will plan the old mattress's pickup so the bedroom is clear before the new one arrives.

Do you take the box spring too, or just the mattress?

Both, as one pickup. They are carried out together and handled the same way, whether that is down two flights or out a ground-floor bedroom.
