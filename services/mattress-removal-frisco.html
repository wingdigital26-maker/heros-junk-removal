What a mattress removal pickup actually involves

Mattress removal in a transient, fast-growing city like Frisco is mostly a timing problem, not a condition problem. A family moving into a new build often has a mattress from the old house that does not match the new bedroom's setup, or a delivery is scheduled and the old mattress has to be gone before the new one arrives. Texas does not run a statewide mattress recycling program, so a mattress that cannot be donated gets separated for its recyclable steel coils and foam where a processor accepts it, rather than defaulting straight to the landfill.
