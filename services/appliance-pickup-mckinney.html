Appliance Pickup, McKinney

McKinney's appliance calls split the same way the rest of the city does. An early-1900s house near the square usually means an appliance that has simply run out its life in a tight original utility space, while a Stonebridge Ranch or Craig Ranch call is more often a working unit coming out during a kitchen remodel.

What an appliance pickup actually involves

A dead water heater or fridge in one of the historic homes near the McKinney square usually sits in a utility closet or kitchen footprint built decades before modern appliance sizes existed, so the crew checks the doorway and closet width before pulling anything loose rather than forcing it. Out west in the newer master-planned sections, the units coming out are often still working, just replaced ahead of a remodel, and get the same basic disconnection either way: unplugging, water lines, and the dryer vent are handled on the spot, while a gas line needs a plumber's cap first and a hardwired unit needs an electrician before we take it from there. Refrigerant units go to certified recyclers who recover the refrigerant before the steel is reclaimed.

What makes McKinney different

The historic neighborhoods around the McKinney square have narrow streets, mature trees, and some alley-only access, which matters for an appliance dolly the same way it matters for furniture: the crew checks whether the alley or the front street actually gets a fridge or washer to the trailer without a fight. West of downtown in Stonebridge Ranch and Craig Ranch, access is easy but the HOA rules on curbside timing are stricter, so a swapped-out appliance gets loaded and hauled the same visit instead of sitting at the curb.

Frequently asked questions

Can an old water heater fit out of a historic McKinney house?

Usually yes, once the crew checks the utility closet width. Have the gas or water lines capped by a licensed plumber first; we handle the dolly and the carry from there.

Do you take a still-working appliance from a Stonebridge Ranch or Craig Ranch remodel?

Yes. A unit swapped out for a kitchen update goes to local donation partners first if it still works; only what no longer runs goes to a metal recycler.

Will an alley near downtown McKinney help or hurt an appliance pickup?

It can help. A lot of the blocks near the square are alley-served, which sometimes puts the trailer closer to a back utility door than the street would. Tell us the layout when you write.
