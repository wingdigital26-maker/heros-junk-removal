The short answer

The big backyard three, handled in one visit

These are the jobs that stall for years because they feel like projects. They are, just not yours. Hero’s crews cut out hot tubs, tear down sheds, and unbolt playsets across DFW in a single scheduled visit, then load everything and haul it off. You watch from the porch with something cold.

How each one goes

Hot tubs

Drain it the day before and have an electrician kill and cap the circuit. The crew walks it out on dollies if the gate allows, or cuts it into sections right in place, which sounds dramatic and takes about an hour. Usually a morning of work. Pool and spa chemicals left in the shed do not ride along, they go to a household chemical collection program, free for residents.

Sheds

Contents cleared, panels unbolted or cut, frame dropped and hauled. Metal or wood both work, and a typical backyard shed is usually a morning of work.

Playsets and trampolines

Unbolted, cut where rusted, metal separated for recycling through the yards listed in the TCEQ recycling directory. Good-condition wood sets can sometimes be rehomed with lead time. The lightest of the three jobs.

Full walkthrough of how each teardown runs: the hot tub, shed, and playset guide. The brush and cut limbs the teardown exposes come out under yard debris, a shed full of old material is closer to a garage cleanout, and what we will and will not take covers the rest.

Bundle the backyard: tub plus shed plus the pile behind the shed goes better as one truck visit than three separate calls, and doing it together works out in your favor. If only the loose pile is left and you are not in a hurry, Plano residents can also set it out for bulky waste collection on the city calendar.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to drain the hot tub first?

Yes, ideally the day before. We can pump the last few inches, but a full tub cannot be moved or cut safely.

Who disconnects the power?

An electrician kills and caps the circuit first. We take it from there. Electrical work stays with electricians.

Do you remove shed contents too?

Yes, quoted as part of the same job, or clear it yourself first if you would rather.

Can the concrete pad go too?

Pad demo is quoted separately because concrete has to be handled as heavy material. Ask during the walkthrough.
