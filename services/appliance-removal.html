The short answer

Any appliance, from any room, recycled right

Hero’s removes every common household appliance across DFW: refrigerators and freezers, washers and dryers, dishwashers, stoves and ovens, water heaters, microwaves, and window AC units. The crew brings the dolly and the floor protection, handles the basic disconnection, and gets it out of the kitchen, the garage, or the upstairs laundry closet without drama. Old appliances are mostly steel, so nearly all of them can skip the landfill entirely and go to metal recyclers instead. The state keeps a TCEQ recycling directory if you would rather run a unit somewhere yourself, and old TVs, printers, and computers follow the separate EPA electronics donation and recycling route.

How pricing works (no surprises)

  • Single appliance: a simple flat price quoted over the phone, hauled from anywhere in the house.
  • Refrigerant units: fridges, freezers, and AC units carry a small surcharge for certified refrigerant recovery. We tell you upfront, not at the truck.
  • Multiple appliances or a remodel's worth: the crew looks at what is coming out and quotes it firm, approved before we load.

More on how the service works: How junk removal works in DFW. If the appliances are coming out with everything else in the room, that is usually a garage cleanout or an estate cleanout instead, and what we will and will not take covers the rest of the list.

What is included in every pickup

  • Two-person crew with appliance dolly and floor runners
  • Simple disconnections: plug, water supply lines, dryer vent
  • Door removal and rehang when the fridge will not clear the frame
  • Certified refrigerant recovery for fridges, freezers, and AC units
  • Donation routing for clean, working units, often to a Habitat ReStore location that accepts working appliances

Gas or hardwired? Have the gas line capped by a plumber or the circuit disconnected by an electrician first, then we handle everything from there. Not sure which you have? Mention the appliance when you book a pickup and we will walk you through it.

Frequently asked questions

Do you take refrigerators and freezers?

Yes. They go to certified recyclers who recover the refrigerant first, which is why they carry a small surcharge. Empty and defrost before pickup if you can.

Do you disconnect appliances before hauling?

Simple disconnections, yes: unplugging, water lines, dryer vents. Gas lines need a plumber's cap and hardwired units an electrician first; we take it from there.

How is appliance removal quoted in DFW?

A single unit gets hauled from wherever it sits, upstairs included. Fridges and freezers need freon recovery, which we handle. Multiple units are quoted firm on sight, always approved upfront.

Can working appliances be donated?

Yes. Clean, working units go to donation partners first, and we can provide a receipt on request.
