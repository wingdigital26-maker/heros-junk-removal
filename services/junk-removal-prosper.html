What a Prosper house actually produces

Prosper has grown faster than almost anywhere else in Collin County, and most of that growth is still fresh. Neighborhoods like Windsong Ranch, Star Trail, and Whitley Place were built out within the last several years, and a lot of what we haul out of them is tied directly to that newness: a new-build move-in that leaves a garage full of flattened boxes and packing foam, a builder or subcontractor's leftover trim and paint cans still sitting in a corner, or a family upgrading a starter appliance package before the warranty on the old one has even run out.

The jobs Prosper generates more than most other towns we work:

  • Move-in and move-out clearing. Boxes, packing material, and furniture that did not make the cut for the new floor plan. That overlaps with furniture removal.
  • Builder and remodel leftovers. Trim scraps, cabinet boxes, and packaging left in a garage after closing, or a kitchen upgrade a few years in. That falls under construction debris and garage cleanouts.
  • New-appliance swaps. A builder-grade appliance replaced early, or one that failed young. That is covered under appliance removal.

New streets, active construction, one thing to flag

A lot of Prosper is still being built out, which means wide new streets and standard driveway gates in the finished sections, but it also means active construction zones nearby that can affect the route in on a given day. Most of Prosper's newer neighborhoods carry active HOAs with rules about how long anything can sit visible from the street, so we plan to load and leave the same visit rather than stage a pile ahead of time. If a street is still unpaved or a gate code is required, tell us when you write and we will plan the approach.

Use the town first when it will do it free

Prosper contracts residential trash and recycling collection, and if your load fits inside that service, use it. We would rather tell you the truth and lose the small job than take money you did not have to spend.

  • Paint, stain, solvents, motor oil, gasoline, pool chemicals, pesticides, and propane tanks never ride on our trailer, and no licensed hauler can take them. Those belong in the Town's own household hazardous waste program, not this pickup.

Where the contracted service stops working is volume and timing. A whole garage, a full house, or anything with a closing date attached is where we come in. The statewide rules behind solid waste handling sit with the TCEQ.
