Appliance Pickup, Plano

Plano's housing stock is decades old in a lot of neighborhoods, which means original or first-replacement appliances are reaching the end of their run at roughly the same time across whole blocks. We dolly the unit out, handle the simple disconnection, and send it to a metal recycler instead of a landfill.

What an appliance pickup actually involves

An appliance pickup is rarely just the appliance. In a Plano house built in the 1970s or 1980s, a failed water heater usually means a narrow original utility closet built for a smaller tank, and a dead fridge often means a kitchen doorway or hallway that was not cut with a modern-depth refrigerator in mind. The crew handles the basic disconnection (unplugging, water lines, the dryer vent) on the spot; gas lines need a plumber's cap first and hardwired units need an electrician, and we take it from there once that step is done. Refrigerant units go to certified recyclers who recover the refrigerant before the steel is reclaimed.

What makes Plano different

Plano runs its own household chemical collection program, which is the right stop for anything that leaked from an old appliance, like used motor oil or old batteries from a garage fridge, since we cannot carry any of that on the trailer. A lot of the pickups we get near Historic Downtown Plano and the older neighborhoods east of Central are original-owner units that have simply run their full life, while the newer Legacy corridor calls are more often a kitchen remodel swapping out a working but dated appliance.

Frequently asked questions

Will an old water heater fit out of a Plano utility closet?

Usually yes, and if the closet is unusually narrow the crew figures out the angle before pulling anything loose. Have the gas or water lines capped by a licensed plumber first; we take it from there.

Does Plano's household chemical program matter for an appliance pickup?

It can. Anything that leaked from an old fridge or a garage freezer, like old batteries or automotive fluid stored nearby, has to go through Plano's household chemical collection program instead of riding on our trailer.

Can a still-working appliance be donated instead of scrapped?

Yes. Clean, working units go to local donation partners first, and units that no longer work are routed to metal recyclers.

Plano is inside our core area, so a call before noon typically means the same afternoon. Typical rather than guaranteed, since access and job size are real variables.
