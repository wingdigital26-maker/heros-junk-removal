Appliance Pickup, Prosper

Prosper's appliance calls are almost always about newness, not age. A builder-grade unit is swapped out for something bigger or better within a few years of move-in, or an original appliance fails well before it should have. We dolly the unit out, handle the basic disconnection, and send it to a metal recycler rather than a landfill.

What an appliance pickup actually involves

An appliance pickup in a Prosper new build is rarely about a unit that simply wore out. More often it is a builder-grade fridge or washer being upgraded within the first few years, or a unit that failed early and is still under some warranty coverage the homeowner is separately handling. The crew takes care of the basic disconnection on the spot: unplugging, water lines, and the dryer vent. A gas line needs a plumber's cap first and a hardwired unit needs an electrician before we take it from there. Refrigerant units go to certified recyclers who recover the refrigerant before the steel is reclaimed.

What makes Prosper different

A lot of Prosper's growth is recent enough that appliance calls skew toward upgrades rather than end-of-life failures, especially in newer sections like Windsong Ranch and Star Trail where move-ins are still happening block by block. Prosper contracts its residential trash and recycling service, and anything that leaked from an old unit, like used oil or old batteries, belongs in the Town's separate household hazardous waste program instead, since none of that rides on our trailer.

Why would a builder-grade appliance need replacing so soon in Prosper?

It is common. A lot of what we pick up is an early upgrade rather than a true failure, since a builder-grade unit is often swapped for something bigger within the first few years of a new build.

Yes. Clean, working units go to local donation partners first, and units that no longer work are routed to metal recyclers.
