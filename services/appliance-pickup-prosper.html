Appliance Pickup, Prosper

Prosper's appliance calls are almost always about newness, not age. We dolly the unit out, handle the basic disconnection, and send it to a metal recycler rather than a landfill.

What an appliance pickup actually involves

An appliance pickup in a Prosper new build is rarely about a unit that simply wore out. More often it is a builder-grade fridge or washer being upgraded within the first few years, or a unit that failed early and is still under some warranty coverage the homeowner is separately handling. The crew takes care of the basic disconnection on the spot: unplugging, water lines, and the dryer vent. A gas line needs a plumber's cap first and a hardwired unit needs an electrician before we take it from there. Refrigerant units go to certified recyclers who recover the refrigerant before the steel is reclaimed.
