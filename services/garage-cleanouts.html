What is included in every cleanout

  • Two-person crew, dollies, and bins for loose items
  • Point-and-go sorting, or hands-off hauling of a pre-sorted go pile
  • Donation routing for tools, bikes, and gear that still work
  • Metal, cardboard, and e-waste separated for recycling

One heads-up: paint, motor oil, gasoline, propane, and pesticides cannot ride on the truck under Texas disposal rules. Most DFW cities take them free at household hazardous waste centers. Dallas runs the Home Chemical Collection Center, Plano has household chemical collection, and the EPA explains why these are handled separately. We will point you to your city's program when we quote the job.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a garage cleanout take?

Most take one to three hours with a two-person crew, including loading and haul-off. Heavily packed garages run longer, and we give you a realistic window on sight.

Do I need to sort before the crew arrives?

No. Pointing works. Pre-sorting into keep and go areas speeds loading.

What garage items can you not take?

Household hazardous waste: paint, oil, antifreeze, gasoline, propane, pesticides. Your city's hazardous waste program takes them, usually free.

The crew walks the garage with you, sees what is going, and gives you a firm number on the spot. Always approved upfront before anything is loaded.
