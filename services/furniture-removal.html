Any furniture, any floor, usually same-day

Hero’s removes every kind of household furniture across DFW: couches and sectionals, mattresses and box springs, dressers, wardrobes, dining sets, desks, entertainment centers, patio sets, and the exercise equipment that became a clothes rack. Our crews come to the furniture, so nothing has to be dragged to the garage or curb first. Call in the morning and most single-item and small pickups happen the same afternoon in Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Allen, and across North Texas.

How pricing works (no surprises)

  • Single items: a couch, mattress, or recliner is a simple flat price, quoted over the phone.
  • Multiple pieces: the crew looks at everything going out and gives you one firm number. You approve it before we lift anything.
  • Stairs, disassembly, tight corners: included. If a sleeper sofa has to come apart to leave the bedroom, that is our problem, not an add-on.

More on how the service works: How junk removal works in DFW. If the whole house is being cleared rather than one room, start at estate cleanouts, and if the pieces are stacked in the garage, garage cleanouts is the closer fit.

What is included in every pickup

  • Two-person crew that does all the lifting and carrying
  • Floor runners and door frame protection on the carry path
  • Disassembly of beds, sectionals, and sleeper sofas when needed
  • Donation runs for usable pieces, often to the Dallas Area Habitat ReStore or another local partner, with receipts available on request

Not everything has to go on a trailer. If a piece is clean and intact, our DFW donation guide shows who takes what, and Habitat keeps a national ReStore directory for finding the closest drop-off. If it can wait for the curb, Dallas collects brush and bulky items on a monthly schedule.

Replacing furniture? Schedule us for delivery day. We haul the old set out in the morning so the new one has a clear landing spot by afternoon. Mention the timing when you book a pickup.

Frequently asked questions

How is furniture removal quoted in DFW?

The crew looks at the pieces on sight, whether it is one couch or a houseful, and gives you a firm number. Every job is quoted upfront before loading starts.

Do you take furniture from upstairs or inside the house?

Yes. We come to wherever it sits, including third-floor apartments, and protect floors and door frames on the way out.

Can my old furniture be donated instead of dumped?

If it is clean and usable, that is exactly what happens. We route donatable pieces to local charity partners first and recycle what we can of the rest.

Do you offer same-day furniture pickup?

Usually yes. Call before noon and we can typically be there the same afternoon, depending on route availability.
