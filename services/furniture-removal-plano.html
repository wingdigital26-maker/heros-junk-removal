What a furniture removal pickup actually involves

Furniture that has sat in the same Plano house since the 1970s or 1980s is a different job than a single sectional: it is usually a living room, a dining set, and a couple of bedrooms all going at once, either because the house is being sold or because a downsizing family is clearing rooms one at a time. Older case pieces, like solid wood dressers and hutches, are heavier than modern furniture and often have to come apart or tilt through a doorway that was standard for its decade but tight by today's sizes. Near the Legacy corridor, the calls skew newer and are more often a single remodel swap, a sectional or sleeper replaced ahead of a listing, and get the same disassembly treatment at the connector brackets or frame joints.
